Put your brand on every link you share. With a custom domain, your short links live on a domain you own, so instead of `spoo.me/xyz` you share links like `ac.me/launch`. Same analytics, same management, your name on the door.

<Note>
  Custom domain registration is being rolled out gradually, so it may not be available on your account yet. Managing already-connected domains is never restricted.
</Note>

## Add your domain

Head to **Domains** in the dashboard and click **Add domain**. Type the domain you want your links on and register it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/custom-domains/add-domain.png" alt="Add a custom domain dialog with ac.me entered" />
</Frame>

## Point DNS at spoo.me

After registering, the domain page walks you through setup step by step. You publish two DNS records at your provider: a CNAME that routes traffic, and a TXT record that proves you own the domain. Copy buttons are right there next to each value.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/custom-domains/dns-records.png" alt="Guided domain setup showing the CNAME and TXT records to add at your DNS provider" />
</Frame>

Once the records resolve, verification and TLS issuance complete automatically. You can also nudge it with **Start verification** if you are impatient. When the badge flips to **Active**, links on your domain start resolving, HTTPS included, no certificates to manage.

## Create branded links

From then on, your domain shows up in the domain picker whenever you create a link. Pick it, choose an alias, done: `ac.me/launch` is live.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/custom-domains/new-link-on-domain.png" alt="New link dialog creating ac.me/launch" />
</Frame>

Links on your domain behave exactly like links on spoo.me: full analytics, password protection, expiration, max clicks, everything.

## Routing you control

Each domain has its own routing settings, so the domain feels like yours even outside of short links:

- **Root redirect**: where visitors land when they open the bare domain
- **Not-found redirect**: a fallback for typos and missing aliases
- **Custom robots.txt**: control how crawlers treat your domain

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/custom-domains/domain-active.png" alt="Active domain page with routing settings and DNS records" />
</Frame>

Leave any of these blank and sensible defaults apply. Keep the DNS records in place though; removing them takes the domain offline.

## Removing a domain

The danger zone at the bottom of the domain page lets you revoke a domain. Links on it stop resolving immediately, and revocation is terminal. You can permanently delete a revoked domain afterwards.

## Good to know

- Your account email must be verified before you can register a domain.
- You can connect multiple domains and move existing links between them.
- Abuse of redirects gets a domain revoked; the routing settings are a feature, not a loophole.
